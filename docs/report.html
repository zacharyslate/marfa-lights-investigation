Report a sighting

A careful record is how an unexplained light becomes evidence. This form takes a few minutes. As you fill it in, it checks your light against every known source in the view.

Write your own account first. If you were with others, fill this in before you compare notes. People who talk before writing tend to converge on each other's details, and then two witnesses count as one. Your report stays on this device until you choose to download it or send it.

Automatic noise check

Enter a bearing to see which known light sources lie in that direction.

This compares your bearing and height with the project's map of roads, rail, lit towers, towns and the aerostat. A match doesn't mean your light was ordinary, and no match doesn't mean it wasn't. It shows which ordinary causes to rule out first.
