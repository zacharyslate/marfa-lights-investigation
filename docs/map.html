What am I looking at?

Identify a light

Enter the compass bearing of a light you see from the Viewing Area, or tap the map or the panorama below. The tool lists every known light source on that line and, for roads, how bright a car there would look.

    View from the platform

    Panorama

    The skyline, roads, towers and railroad as seen from the Viewing Area, south-southeast (left) to west-northwest (right). Green is the Zone of Skepticism: places where a known light source can appear. Tap where you see the light, relative to the mountains, to set both bearing and elevation.

    Map layers

    Layers and refraction

    At k = 0.13 the roads show the standard classes. Move the slider to see which stretches a headlight could be seen from under other conditions. Clear, calm nights with a surface temperature inversion raise k.
