Mitchell Flat · Presidio County, Texas

Know what you're looking at

Since at least the 1880s, people have watched unexplained lights over the flat southeast of Marfa. Most lights seen from the Viewing Area have ordinary sources: cars, trains, towers, towns. This guide helps you rule those out, so what's left is worth your attention.
