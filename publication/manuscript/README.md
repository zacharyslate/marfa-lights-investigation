# Manuscript: American Journal of Physics (model paper)

*Where headlights can hide: line of sight, refraction, and photometry of distant car lights at Marfa, Texas.*

- `marfa_ajp.tex`: REVTeX 4.2, two-column. Figures are read from `../figures/` and are regenerated by `analysis/pub_figures.py`, `analysis/photo_figures.py` and `analysis/manuscript_figures.py`.
- `refs.bib`: every entry was checked against its source.
- Build: `latexmk -pdf marfa_ajp.tex`.
- Anonymous copy for review: `latexmk -pdf -jobname=marfa_ajp_anon -pdflatex='pdflatex %O "\def\anonbuild{1}\input{%S}"' marfa_ajp.tex`. This withholds the author, affiliation and repository URL.

## Before submission (author)

1. **Length.** The draft runs to 10 two-column pages. AJP's norm for regular papers is about 6 pages ([formatting guide](https://www.aapt.org/Publications/AJP/Contributors/Formatting_the_manuscript.cfm)). Candidates to cut or move to supplementary material:
   - the refraction figure (Fig. 5);
   - Table IV (ray tracing), which could be summarised in two sentences;
   - Appendix C.
2. **Affiliation and acknowledgments.** Both are placeholders.
3. **AI-use statement.** This is required by AIP Publishing when AI affects analysis or data extraction ([policy](https://publishing.aip.org/resources/researchers/policies-and-ethics/ai-policy/)). Review the wording and confirm the tool and version.
4. **Photographs.** Credit: Zach Warren. Decide whether to deposit the raw frames as supplementary material.
5. **Field experiments** are deliberately left out (author decision, 2026-09-29). The design is in `../notes/12_experimental_design.md`.
