# Manuscript: American Journal of Physics (model paper)

*Separating the known from the unknown at Marfa, Texas: line of sight, refraction, and photometry of the ordinary lights seen from the Marfa lights viewing area.* Framed as a screening tool: it removes false positives (headlamps, tail lamps, trains, towers, skyglow) and defines the observables that would make a light unexplained.

- `marfa_ajp.tex`: REVTeX 4.2, two-column. Figures are read from `../figures/` and are regenerated by `analysis/pub_figures.py`, `analysis/photo_figures.py` and `analysis/manuscript_figures.py`.
- `refs.bib`, `refs_si.bib`: every entry was checked against its source.
- `marfa_ajp_SI.tex`: supplementary material, with a table of contents. It holds the full methods (formerly the appendices), additional tables and figures S1–S3, and a guide to the website. Build: `latexmk -pdf marfa_ajp_SI.tex`.
- Build: `latexmk -pdf marfa_ajp.tex`.
- Anonymous copies for review:
  - `latexmk -pdf -jobname=marfa_ajp_anon -pdflatex='pdflatex %O "\def\anonbuild{1}\input{%S}"' marfa_ajp.tex`
  - and the same with `marfa_ajp_SI_anon` / `marfa_ajp_SI.tex`.
  - These withhold the author, affiliation, repository and website URLs.
